HOW WE CAN HELP

Support to get more from your NDIS plan.

Navigating the NDIS can sometimes feel complicated. At Altora Care, we work alongside you to make your plan easier to understand and help you make informed decisions about your supports.

Our Support Coordination is tailored to your individual needs, circumstances and goals. We can help you explore your options, connect with providers and build a support network that works for you.

We focus on giving you greater choice and control while helping you build the confidence and skills to manage your supports over time.

WHAT WE CAN SUPPORT WITH

Practical support, tailored to you.

01

Understanding Your Plan

Helping you understand your NDIS funding, support categories and how your plan can be used.

02

Connecting With Providers

Helping you identify and connect with providers and services that suit your needs and preferences.

03

Coordinating Your Supports

Helping your different services work together so your supports are organised and focused on your goals.

04

Building Your Independence

Supporting you to develop the skills and confidence to make decisions and manage your supports more independently.

05

Preparing for Plan Reviews

Helping you prepare for NDIS plan reassessments by reviewing your current supports, goals and changing circumstances.

06

Responding to Changes

Helping you navigate changes in your circumstances and identify appropriate supports when your needs change.

OUR APPROACH

Your goals guide the way.

We understand that no two participants have the same goals or circumstances.

We take the time to understand what is important to you and work with you to create a support network that reflects your individual needs, choices and lifestyle.

Our role is to provide guidance and support while ensuring that you remain at the centre of every decision.
