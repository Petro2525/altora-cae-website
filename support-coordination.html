WHAT IS SUPPORT COORDINATION?

Understanding your plan doesn't have to feel overwhelming.

We're here to help make things clearer.

Support Coordination is designed to help you understand and make effective use of the supports available in your NDIS plan.

Your Support Coordinator works alongside you to understand your goals, identify suitable services, connect with providers and help coordinate the different supports in your life.

The aim isn't simply to organise services for you. It's also to build your confidence, knowledge and ability to make informed decisions about your supports.

At Altora Care, we believe you should remain at the centre of every decision about your plan.
